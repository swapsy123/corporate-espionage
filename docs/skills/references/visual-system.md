# Reference visual system

The 11 JPEGs in examples/ are the user-supplied reference post, ordered as supplied. They illustrate design only; neither their depicted identity nor their copy belongs in new output.

## Shared geometry

Canvas: 1080 × 1350, 4:5. Photos bleed to all edges. Start with 65–85 px side padding and 80–110 px top padding. Use white type, generous spacing, and a subtly darkened background or directional black gradient. No red KingKeto branding, neon, heavy cards, or oversized logos by default: KingKeto specifies the writing voice here; the attached references specify the appearance.

Headlines: high-contrast editorial serif, roughly 90–118 px, close leading; actual size depends on length. Use real italics for a meaningful phrase. Body: clean sans-serif, roughly 30–36 px with 1.25–1.4 leading. Takeaways: serif/italic around 40–48 px or selective bold sans-serif. These are starting points, not a reason to squeeze text. Rewrite or split dense content before shrinking it excessively. Thin white rules are approximately 190 px wide and 2 px high.

## Reusable layouts

1. **Collage cover — example 01:** four unique photos in a flush 2 × 2 grid, each panel 540 × 675. Center a large serif hook across the middle, with italic emphasis. Keep the headline concise and protect faces when cropping. Apply sufficient contrast across panel seams.
2. **Lesson — examples 02–05:** single full-bleed photo, headline at upper left, a short explanation in a narrower left column, optional rule, then a short actionable takeaway. Photo subject generally occupies the right or lower portion. Place text according to the actual subject, rather than blindly copying coordinates.
3. **Three actions — example 06:** headline followed by three concise rows, subtle separators, optional simple outline icons, and a serif takeaway. Icons clarify content and remain subordinate to the photography.
4. **Contrast or paired examples — examples 07–08:** two compact statements separated by rules, followed by one concluding idea. Preserve ample vertical breathing room.
5. **Statement — examples 09–10:** large headline and two short supporting passages; optional italic subheading. Keep the background photo prominent.
6. **Closing collage — example 11:** four new unique photos, centered serif close and CTA. Adapt to KingKeto and the actual post. Use a follow CTA unless the user supplies another CTA; do not reproduce the example creator's name or generic introduction verbatim.

A single-photo cover or closing slide is a useful explicit adaptation when photo supply or subject matter makes a collage unsuitable. Calculate and communicate the changed photo count.

## Content rhythm

Vary the layout only when the idea warrants it. A lesson typically needs a short headline, 20–55 words of explanation, and optionally a short takeaway. The example's composition and hierarchy matter more than matching every line break. Let both the photo and the idea determine the final layout.
